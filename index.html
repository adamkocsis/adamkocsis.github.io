Hi and welcome to my personal website! This is me, Adam — a random guy with an affinity to natural philosophy and with scientific activities in the intersecting areas of paleontology, geology and biogeography/macroecology. My primary interests (details) target Earth System-level processes in order to better understand the role and strucutre of life and how it has been coevolving with the planet on a global scale. I work at the GeoZentrum Nordbayern division Friedrich-Alexander University of Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), where I conduct research, develop and maintiain computational resources, and teach from the undergraduate to the PhD-level.
